# MCL

Label 1 when the report supports a clinically meaningful MCL injury.

## Positive
- Explicit MCL tear or rupture.
- Partial-thickness or complete MCL tear.
- Explicit grade 2 or grade 3 MCL sprain/injury, even if some fibers remain continuous.
- Loss of continuity or definite structural injury of superficial/deep MCL fibers.
- A final impression naming grade 2/3 MCL injury is sufficient positive evidence.

## Negative
- MCL normal, intact, preserved.
- Grade 1 sprain/injury only.
- Periligamentous edema or fluid alone.
- Enthesopathic/degenerative thickening without an injury diagnosis.
- MPFL, medial retinaculum, or other medial structures are not MCL.

## Conflict rule
Prefer the final impression/conclusion over isolated mild signal descriptions when they disagree.
