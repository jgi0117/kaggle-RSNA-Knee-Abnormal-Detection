# ACL

Label 1 when the report supports a true ACL tear/rupture or a radiologist-designated clinically meaningful ACL injury.

## Positive
- Explicit ACL tear or rupture: complete, near-complete, high-grade partial, partial-thickness, interstitial/intrasubstance tear.
- Fiber discontinuity, loss of continuity, nonvisualization, or avulsion when interpreted as ACL injury.
- Explicit grade 2 or grade 3 ACL injury/sprain, especially when the impression lists ACL injury.
- A specific ACL tear in the impression/conclusion should outweigh a vague earlier description of increased signal.

## Negative
- ACL normal, intact, preserved, or no tear.
- Mucoid degeneration, cystic change, or increased signal with preserved continuity and no tear diagnosis.
- Grade 1 injury/sprain without structural disruption.
- PCL/MCL/LCL/MPFL abnormalities do not count.

## Conflict rule
If findings and impression conflict, prefer the more specific final impression/conclusion. Do not infer ACL injury from indirect signs alone.
