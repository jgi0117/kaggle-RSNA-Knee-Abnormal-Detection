# Effusion

Label 1 when the report explicitly identifies an intra-articular knee joint effusion.

## Positive
- Joint effusion explicitly present, regardless of whether described as minimal, mild, small, moderate, large, massive, or hydrops.
- Increased intra-articular joint fluid / increased fluid in the knee joint space.
- Hemarthrosis when it represents intra-articular joint fluid.

## Negative
- Explicit no joint effusion / no significant knee effusion.
- Physiologic fluid only, if explicitly described as physiologic.
- Fluid limited to a bursa, Baker cyst, or soft tissues without joint effusion.

## Priority rule
For this dataset, do not require a moderate-or-greater severity threshold. An explicit "mild/small/minimal joint effusion" is usually sufficient for label 1.
