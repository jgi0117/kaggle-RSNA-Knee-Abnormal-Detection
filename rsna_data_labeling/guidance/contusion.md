# Contusion

Label 1 for a traumatic bone contusion / bone bruise pattern.

## Positive
- Explicit bone contusion / bone bruise.
- Contusional or traumatic bone marrow edema when presented as a separate traumatic lesion.
- Kissing contusions or pivot-shift contusion pattern.
- Traumatic marrow edema not fully explained by fracture or degeneration.

## Negative
- Degenerative/subchondral marrow edema from OA/chondrosis.
- Marrow edema entirely attributable to a fracture, unless a separate bone bruise/contusion is also described.
- Soft-tissue or muscle contusion when the target is bone contusion.
- Nonspecific edema without traumatic interpretation.
- Explicit no bone bruise/contusion.

## Caution
If fracture and contusion are both separately named, both may be positive. If only fracture-associated edema is described, do not automatically create a separate Contusion label.
